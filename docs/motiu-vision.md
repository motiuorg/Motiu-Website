# Motiu — vision, mission, vibe

Source of truth: this document + the editorial notes of 2026-09-03. Where the site copy
disagrees with these notes, the notes win — the site was drafted before the positioning
below was decided.

## Who we are

Motiu is a cooperative-in-formation in Barcelona (Bloc4), rooted in the Economia Social i
Solidària tradition. We work on the **connective layer of territorial regeneration**: the
financial strategies, shared knowledge, and coordination that let networks of initiatives
act coherently across their territory. We are not a technology company, not a blockchain
project, and not a federation — we are operators grounded in Catalonia, working with
landscape-scale partners in Europe and beyond.

"Motiu" = motive, reason, the thing that moves people to act. That is the position we take:
we work on the *motives and the mechanisms* that make regeneration compound.

## The problem we address (framing we use)

Regenerative work fails when its parts run on separate tracks: projects that should
reinforce each other operate in isolation; ecological and social goals are run as separate
agendas; the financial systems available were designed for a different era and cannot
follow a transition through the decades it takes. We work on those conditions — the
strategies, knowledge systems, and coordination *between* initiatives — because that is
where regeneration either compounds or stalls.

**Banned framings** (from the notes):

- "we build" as our dominant verb — we *design and run*; building is a means, not the
  identity
- the "gap at the heart" / "infrastructure" centering of the old approach copy — vague

  equally
- any web3/blockchain vocabulary (Safe, Gitcoin, multi-sig, on-chain, DAO, tokens…)

## What we do, concretely (canonical list)

1. **BioFi / landscape-scale finance strategies** — design, execution, and coordination
   of landscape-based financial strategies (work with Commonland).
2. **Collective knowledge infrastructure** — design and maintenance of linked knowledge
   bases, maps, and dashboards (BWL portfolio mapping, Commonland, Licitacoop, rc2).
3. **Funding & capacity-building programs** — programs that pair capital with capacity,
   designed and run end-to-end (Regenerant Catalunya).
4. **Bioregional network weaving & sensemaking** — mapping and convening the actors of a
   territory so they recognize each other and act as one movement (ongoing in Catalunya).
5. **Collective financial infrastructures** — design and implementation of financial
   vehicles a network owns together, from shared treasuries to bioregional investment
   cooperatives.
6. **Strategy consulting for regenerative projects, processes, and networks** — the
   meta-layer over the above, with a deliberate focus on designing and implementing
   BioFi / landscape-scale financial strategies and improving existing ones.
7. **Movement narratives & strategies** — promoting regeneration and system change as
   an answer to the current questions/problems, presenting our work as one of the best
   answers available.

**Open strategic question** (do not bake into site copy yet): whether we become a
finance-and-knowledge entity for good, or eventually the meta-strategy entity that deals
with all the processes. For now the site speaks to what we practice today: **finance and
knowledge**, wrapped in coordination and capacity building.

## Demoted or removed service language

- "Program facilitation & network accompaniment" — vague; expressed through the lines above.
- "Process alignment & automation" — a secondary skill; we are unlikely to sell it as a
  service; never center it.
- "Grant programs & collaborative funding" — weak/vague; expressed through BioFi strategy
  and funding & capacity-building programs.

## Mission

Design and run the financial strategies, collective knowledge systems, and coordination
that regenerative networks need — and practice it as the thing itself, not as a promise:
every service we name is backed by a program we have actually designed and run.

## Vibe

- **Grounded and proof-first**: case studies are real engagements (Regenerant Catalunya,
  BWL, Commonland, bioregioning.earth, Regenerant Catalunya Maps).
- **Plain-spoken**: no web3, no blockchain, no federation-speak. Regenerative finance,
  knowledge commons, coordination — in ordinary words.
- **Territorial**: Catalonia is the anchor; landscape-scale Europe is the horizon.
- **Cooperative**: governance and ownership stay with the people on the ground; tooling
  serves it, never the other way around.
- **Strategic**: we point at the problem, then show why our approach is one of the good
  answers — narrative as a practice, not marketing.
